Account deletion

How users can delete their account

To delete your BLA Real Estate account and associated personal data, send an email request from your registered email address or include your registered mobile number.

1

Email support

Send your request to support@hisarpropertybazar.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

2

Share account details

Include your full name and registered mobile number so our team can identify the correct account.

3

Confirmation

After verification, we will process the request and confirm once the account deletion has been completed.

Privacy details

Data deletion and retention

Data deleted

Account profile data, registered contact details, preferences, favourites, and personal information associated with your BLA Real Estate account may be deleted after verification.

Data retained when legally required

Some records may be retained if required for legal, security, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or compliance purposes.

Processing time

Deleted account data is removed within 90 days, except where retention is required by law.
