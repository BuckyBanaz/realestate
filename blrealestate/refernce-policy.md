**Privacy Policy**
**Best Evento** | Last updated: March 22, 2026

Best Evento ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Best Evento mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in accordance with Google Play policies (2026) and applicable data protection laws. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

### 1. Age Requirement

This App is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18. If we become aware that such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

---

### 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our services:

* **Name** – voluntarily provided when booking an event
* **Gender** – voluntarily provided for event capacity management
* **Anonymous Device ID** – generated locally to manage bookings and prevent abuse
* **FCM Push Token** – used to send notifications

We collect **limited personal information (such as name and gender)** only when voluntarily provided.
We do **not** collect email address, phone number, precise location, financial data, or contacts.

---

### 3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

* **User consent** (when you provide booking details)
* **Legitimate interest** (to manage event capacity and prevent abuse)

---

### 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

* Manage event reservations
* Enforce event capacity limits
* Send booking confirmations and reminders
* Send event updates via push notifications
* Prevent abuse and duplicate bookings

---

### 5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services:

* **Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Authentication)** – data storage, authentication, and notifications
* **Cloudinary** – image hosting

These services act as **data processors** and process data on our behalf. They do not use your data for advertising purposes.

---

### 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers.
Data is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the App.

---

### 7. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

* Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
* Secure cloud infrastructure (Firebase Firestore)
* Access controls and authentication
* Rate limiting and abuse prevention

---

### 8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for event operations.
Users may request deletion at any time.

---

### 9. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by contacting us via email.

* Deletion requests are processed within **7 days**
* If available, users may also delete their data directly within the app

---

### 10. Push Notifications

The App may send notifications related to bookings and events.
You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

---

### 11. Payments

All payments are made offline at the event venue.
We do not collect or process any financial information.

### 12. Your Rights

You have the right to:

* Access your data
* Request correction or deletion
* Withdraw consent
* Disable notifications

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
